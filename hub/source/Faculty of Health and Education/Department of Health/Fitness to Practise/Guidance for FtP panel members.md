# Fitness to Practise: guidance for panel members

Department of Health. Version 3, October 1052. Read with the Fitness to Practise Procedure (Academic Regulations, Quality).

## What fitness to practise is for

Our regulated programmes lead to registration with a professional body. The University has a duty to be satisfied that a candidate is fit to practise before confirming the award. Fitness to practise is about whether a candidate's health or conduct would put patients, service users or the public at risk. It is not a disciplinary process, though the two can overlap, and it is not a way of managing academic failure.

## What is and is not a referral

Usually referred: dishonesty in any form; breaches of patient confidentiality; working outside one's competence without supervision; behaviour towards patients or colleagues that would concern the professional body; failure to disclose something that must be disclosed; a health condition the candidate is not managing.

Usually not referred: an honest mistake reported promptly; academic failure; a single lapse in professionalism dealt with on the day by the mentor; a health condition the candidate is managing with support.

The most common referral in this Department is a documentation error on placement. Most are honest. The question for the panel is almost always what the candidate did next.

## Your papers

You will receive a case summary from the Lead, normally a week before the panel, by email to you personally. It is Confidential. Read it, bring it, and destroy it afterwards, including the email. Do not save it to a shared folder, a team folder or a module file. Do not forward it. Panel papers have twice been found in shared folders in this Faculty and each time it was a panel member who meant well.

## At the panel

The candidate may bring a companion. The panel hears the Lead's summary, the candidate, and any witnesses. The panel deliberates in private. Outcomes range from no action to removal from the programme; most sit in the middle: a warning, conditions on return, a reflective account.

## Afterwards

The Lead writes to the candidate. The outcome is recorded on the candidate's file by the Lead and nowhere else. Panel members retain nothing.
