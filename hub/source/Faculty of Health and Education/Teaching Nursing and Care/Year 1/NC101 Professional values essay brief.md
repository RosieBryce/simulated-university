# NC101: Professional values essay

1,500 words. 40% of the module. Deadline week 8, Friday, 16:00, through the submission portal.

## The question

"The most important thing a nurse does is notice." Discuss, with reference to the professional code and to one of the case studies on the module page.

## What we are looking for

- You understand what the code asks of you and can say it in your own words.
- You can connect a principle to a situation: what noticing looks like at a bedside, in a handover, on a night shift.
- You can see more than one side. The quotation is not the whole truth.
- You write clearly. Short sentences are fine. Say what you mean.

## What we are not looking for

- Long quotations from the code. Paraphrase.
- A list of values with a sentence each.
- Anything about a real patient, colleague or placement area.

## Marking

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Understanding of the professional code | 30% |
| Application to the case study | 30% |
| Argument and balance | 25% |
| Writing and referencing | 15% |

Students with an adjustments summary: the extension entitlement applies to this essay. Email me; you do not need to give a reason.
