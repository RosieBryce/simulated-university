# MEMORANDUM

**From:** Dr Hrogar Dourstone, Head of the Department of Healing Arts
**To:** The Principal
**Date:** 14.03.49
**Subject:** Car parking

1. I write to record that on three occasions this month a vehicle not belonging to a member of the Department has been found in the space allocated to the Head of Department.

2. I am minded to request that the space be marked more clearly.

3. I should be grateful for the Principal's attention to the matter.

H. Dourstone
