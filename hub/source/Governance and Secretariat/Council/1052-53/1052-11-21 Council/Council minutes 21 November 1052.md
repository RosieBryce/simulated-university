| 12.5 | **Annual report on student outcomes 1051-52** (C/1052/22). The Head of Planning and Insight presented the report, drawing attention to the section on how to read the figures. Council CONSIDERED the report. Members noted the continuing difference in good honours rates between Elf and Dwarf graduates across the University and particularly in the Faculty of Health and Education, where the report gave the difference for 1052 graduates as {{ he_good_degree_gap_1052 }} percentage points. A member asked whether the difference was narrowing. The Head of Planning and Insight said that the gap had moved up and down over five years and that he would be cautious about reading a trend into any single year's movement, in either direction; the five-year table was the better guide. The Chair observed that Council had seen a version of this table for several years and asked what the Faculty was doing. The Vice-Chancellor said that the Dean would be able to say more. Council NOTED the report and ASKED that the Faculty's response be reported to a future meeting. | University Secretary: schedule Faculty response, date to be agreed with the Dean. |

| 12.8 | **StoneShare migration: completion report** (C/1052/25). The Head of Digital Services reported that the legacy StoneShare network drive had been migrated to the Hub over the weekend of 8 and 9 November without loss of data, on a lift-and-shift basis at the request of the Faculty, and that the drive had been decommissioned. A member asked whether material had been reviewed before migration. The Head of Digital Services said that review was for the owning teams and had not been in scope. Council NOTED the report. | |
