# Information governance essentials

Staff training, spring 1053. Information Governance and Digital Services. 45 minutes.

---

# Three labels

- Public: anyone may see it
- Internal: staff, for work
- Confidential: named people who need it, and nobody else
- If it names a person and says something about them, it is Confidential
- No label means Internal, not nothing

---

# Where things go wrong

- The right document to the wrong list
- A copy "for the file" in a folder everyone can open
- A tracker that grew columns it never needed
- A migrated drive nobody has looked at
- A real case used as the example in a guide

---

# Exercise one: the confident summary

- You have read the two-page paper in your pack
- Here is a summary of it produced by the institutional assistant
- Find the four things that are wrong
- Two minutes

---

# What it got wrong

- A figure doubled
- An action given to the wrong person
- A date one month out
- A caveat dropped
- It read well. That is the point.

---

# The AI policy in one slide

- Public: any tool
- Internal: Cloudi only
- Confidential: no tool, no exception without a DPIA
- You are responsible for the output
- Check names, dates, figures, quotations, attribution
- Say when you used it

---

# What Cloudi can and cannot see

- The label on a document opened through the add-in: yes, and it will refuse Confidential
- The content of a document to work out its classification: no
- Text you paste into the chat: no label, no check, your judgement

---

# Misdirected email: the first hour

- Stop: do not send anything else
- Recall, and assume it failed
- Ring 2010
- Do not investigate first
- An honest mistake reported promptly is a learning matter

---

# Containment is not finished when the email is deleted

- Ask where copies went
- Go and look
- Verify removal yourself
- INC-0031: closed on a colleague's "done"; the file was still there

---

# Keeping records

- Name files so a stranger can tell what they are: date, topic, version
- One current version; superseded versions in Superseded
- Retention schedule: when it is due, destroy it
- Email is not a filing system
