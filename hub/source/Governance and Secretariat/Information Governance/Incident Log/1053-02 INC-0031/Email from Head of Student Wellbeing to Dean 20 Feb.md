Subject: Yesterday's incident: what I need from the Faculty

Cyranor

You will have heard from Elenna about yesterday. I want to be clear about three things and then ask you for two.

One of my advisers sent a student's full support plan to fourteen of your staff. He reported it himself within the hour. He is a good adviser who was doing nine of these in a morning because the Faculty asked for all Year 2 summaries before placement preparation started and gave us four working days. I am not excusing the error. I am telling you the conditions it happened in.

The student has been told. I did it myself this morning. She was upset, and then she was angry, and then she asked whether her placement mentor would now know about her anxiety. I said no. I need that to be true.

Elenna has asked everyone to delete it. Two of your staff had already saved it into the Year 2 teaching folder on the Hub for the module file. That is a Confidential document about our student sitting in an Internal folder that every member of the programme team can open.

What I need from you.

First, that the copy in the teaching folder is removed today and that whoever removes it tells Elenna they have done so. Not that they will. That they have.

Second, that the Faculty stops asking for summaries in bulk against a deadline. The procedure says one summary, per student, to the named staff who teach them, when placement preparation begins for that student. Fourteen people on a list is not that.

I am not asking for anyone to be disciplined. I am asking for the file to be gone and for this not to happen again.

Velra
