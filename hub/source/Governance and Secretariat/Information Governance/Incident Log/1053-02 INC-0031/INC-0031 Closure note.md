# INC-0031: Closure note

Information Governance Manager, 7 March 1053. Confidential.

## Summary

On 19 February 1053 a Wellbeing Adviser attached a student's full support plan, instead of the one-page adjustments summary, to an email to the NC-Y2-Teaching distribution list (14 staff). The adviser self-reported within the hour. The incident was contained the same day, the student was informed the following day, and the regulator did not require notification.

## Actions and status

| # | Action | Owner | Status | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deletion requested from all 14 recipients; confirmations received | IGM | Complete, 14 of 14 by 24 Feb | IGM (confirmations on file) |
| 2 | Copy saved to the Year 2 teaching folder on the Hub to be removed | Programme Leader, Nursing and Care | Complete | Programme Leader confirmed by email 27 Feb: "done" |
| 3 | Review meeting held | IGM | Complete, 26 Feb | Note on file (Head of Student Wellbeing) |
| 4 | Sharing procedure revised: summary generated as a separate file, separate folder, distinct name pattern; bulk requests from faculties not accepted | Head of Student Wellbeing | Draft complete 5 Mar; approval at next IGG | IGM |
| 5 | All advisers briefed | Head of Student Wellbeing | Complete, 3 Mar | Head of Student Wellbeing |

## Lessons

- Similar names in the same folder will be confused under time pressure. The fix is structural, not a reminder to be careful.
- Faculties requesting Confidential material in bulk against a deadline creates the conditions for error. The revised procedure declines such requests.
- Recipients saving an attachment "for the file" is the normal thing to do and is the reason containment must include checking where copies have gone.

## Closure

All actions complete or on track with an owner and date. Incident closed 7 March 1053.

E. Dewscroll

*Added to the Data Breach Procedure at version 1.2: removal of copies must be verified by Information Governance, not taken on the owner's confirmation. This incident's action 2 was closed on the Programme Leader's confirmation; under the revised procedure it would be checked directly.*
