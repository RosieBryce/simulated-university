**What we need you to do now**

1. Delete the email and its attachment from your inbox, and then from your deleted items.
2. If you opened or saved the attachment anywhere (your desktop, a Hub folder, a module file), delete that copy too and tell me where it was.
3. Do not forward the email to anyone, including to me.
4. Reply to this message to confirm you have done 1 and 2. A one-word reply is fine.

Please do this today. I will chase on Thursday anyone I have not heard from.

The plan contains health information and personal history that the student shared with their adviser in confidence. The student is being told what has happened. The best thing we can do for them now is to make sure no copy remains.

The colleague who sent it reported it within the hour, which is exactly what our procedure asks. This email is not about fault. It is about containment.
