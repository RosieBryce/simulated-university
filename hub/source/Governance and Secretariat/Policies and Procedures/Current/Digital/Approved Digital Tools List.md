# Approved Digital Tools List

Maintained by Digital Services. Updated 12 February 1053. Tools not on this list are not approved for Internal or Confidential material. Requests to add a tool go through the Digital Services intake process and need a business case.

## Generative and assistive AI

| Tool | Supplier | Approved for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cloudi (chat and office add-in) | Cloudi Systems | Public and Internal | The institutional assistant. Not for Confidential material without a DPIA. See Technical Annex A1. |

## Core productivity

| Tool | Approved for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stonegrove Hub (document libraries, lists) | All classifications, in the correct library | The only approved location for Confidential material |
| Office suite (documents, spreadsheets, slides, mail) | All classifications | Built-in "Summarise" feature is Cloudi and follows the same rules |
| Stonegrove Mail | All classifications | Confidential material to named recipients only, never distribution lists |
| Stonegrove Meet (video calls, recording, transcription) | Internal | Transcripts of meetings with reserved items must be deleted after minutes are approved |

## Data and analysis

| Tool | Approved for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Insight Portal (Planning and Insight dashboards) | Internal | Aggregated data only |
| Statistical workbench (Python and R environments, Digital Services hosted) | Internal | Individual-level extracts only with Planning and Insight agreement |
| Survey tool (Stonegrove Forms) | Internal; Confidential with DPO sign-off per survey | |

## Specifically not approved

| Tool | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGlimmer | Not approved | Consumer service; no contractual data protection; content may be used for training |
| QuillMind | Not approved | As above |
| Summarix browser extension | Not approved | Reads every page you open |
| Personal cloud storage of any kind | Not approved | University material must stay in the Hub |
| Free online file converters and PDF tools | Not approved for Internal or Confidential | Your file is uploaded to a third party |

## Requesting a tool

Submit a Digital Services Project Request through the Hub. Include: what you need to do, why Cloudi does not meet the need, the classification of the material involved, and who will own the tool locally. Requests without a business case are returned.
