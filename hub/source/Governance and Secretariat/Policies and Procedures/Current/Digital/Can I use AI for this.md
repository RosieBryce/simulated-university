# Can I use AI for this?

A one-page guide for staff. It does not replace the AI Acceptable Use Policy; it gets you to the right paragraph of it.

## Start here

**1. What is the classification of the material you want to put in?**

Look at the label on the document or the folder. If there is no label, treat it as Internal. If it names a person and says something about them, treat it as Confidential whatever the label says.

- **Public**: go to 2.
- **Internal**: go to 3.
- **Confidential**: **stop**. You may not use any AI tool with this material. If you believe there is a strong case, speak to the Data Protection Officer about a Data Protection Impact Assessment. Do not start without one.

**2. Public material**

You may use any tool. You are still responsible for the output. Check it. Go to 5.

**3. Internal material: is the tool on the Approved Digital Tools List?**

- **Yes** (at present, Cloudi): go to 4.
- **No**: **stop**. Use the approved tool instead, or do the task without AI.

**4. Does the task involve a decision about a person?**

Admissions, progression, award, disciplinary, fitness to practise, recruitment, grievance, reference.

- **Yes**: the tool may help you draft or summarise, but the decision, and the reasons for it, must be yours and must be recorded as yours. Go to 5.
- **No**: go to 5.

**5. Before you rely on the output**

- Check every name, date, figure and quotation against the source.
- Check that nothing has been attributed to the wrong person.
- Check that nothing has been left out that matters.
- If the output will be read by others, note that AI was used and who checked it.

## Quick answers

| I want to... | Answer |
|---|---|
| Turn a meeting transcript into draft minutes | Yes, with Cloudi, if the meeting had no reserved or confidential items. A named person finalises. |
| Summarise a Council pack for the Dean | Yes, with Cloudi. Check the summary against the pack. |
| Pull provider details out of thirty letters into a table | Yes, with Cloudi. Check a sample of rows against the letters. |
| Tidy up a reference I am writing for a student | **No.** References are Confidential and about a person. |
| Check the grammar in an applicant's personal statement | **No.** Applicant records are Confidential. Not in any tool. |
| Draft a student-facing guide from the regulations | Yes, with any tool: the regulations are Public. Check it against the regulation. |
| Analyse the awarding gap by programme | Yes, with Cloudi, using the aggregated extracts from Planning and Insight. Not the individual-level data. |
| Write a difficult email to a colleague | Yes, with Cloudi, as long as you do not paste in Confidential material about anyone. Then read it as the recipient. |

## Who to ask

Classification and data protection: Information Governance Manager, extension 2010.
Tools and access: Digital Services, extension 5000.
