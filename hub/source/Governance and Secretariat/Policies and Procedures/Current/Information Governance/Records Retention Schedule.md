```sheet:Notes
Note
This schedule was approved by the Information Governance Group on 10 June 1050 and reviewed 1 September 1052.
Retention periods run from the trigger event in the Trigger column.
Where a record is subject to a live complaint or legal hold the period is suspended.
"Destroy" means permanent deletion from the Hub and any copies. "Archive" means transfer to the University Archive.
Teams migrating legacy drives should apply this schedule on arrival. Material past its retention date should not be migrated.
Questions to the Information Governance Manager, extension 2010.
```
