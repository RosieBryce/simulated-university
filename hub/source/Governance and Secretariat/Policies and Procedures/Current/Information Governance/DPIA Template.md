# Data Protection Impact Assessment

Template version 2.0, 12 February 1053. Complete every section. Send to the Data Protection Officer before processing begins. A DPIA is required for any new use of Confidential material with a digital tool, including any AI tool, and for any processing likely to result in a high risk to individuals.

## Part A: The proposal

**A1. Title of the activity**

**A2. Lead and team**

**A3. Describe the processing.** What data, about whom, collected how, used for what, shared with whom, stored where, for how long. Include a data flow diagram if there are more than two systems.

**A4. Why is it needed?** The purpose, and why it cannot be met without personal data or with less of it.

**A5. Tools and suppliers involved.** For each tool: is it on the Approved Digital Tools List; where is data processed; does the supplier retain or train on it.

## Part B: Consultation

**B1. Who has been consulted?** Individuals affected (or their representatives), the owning team, Digital Services, the DPO.

**B2. What did they say and what changed as a result?**

## Part C: Necessity and proportionality

**C1. Lawful basis.**

**C2. Data minimisation.** What is the least data that would do? Why is more needed, if it is?

**C3. Accuracy.** How will errors be found and corrected? For AI tools, how will outputs be checked against sources?

**C4. Retention.** Which line of the Records Retention Schedule applies?

**C5. Rights.** How will individuals be told, and how will access, rectification and objection be handled?

**C6. Decisions about people.** Will any decision affecting an individual be made or influenced by the processing? If so, how is human judgement guaranteed?
