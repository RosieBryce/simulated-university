# Getting support at Stonegrove

Student Wellbeing. For students. 1052-53.

## Who we are

Student Wellbeing is the University's disability, mental health and wellbeing service. We are separate from your department. What you tell us stays with us unless you agree otherwise or someone is at risk.

## What we do

- **Support plans.** If you have a disability, a specific learning difference, a long-term health condition or a mental health condition, we can agree a plan of adjustments: extra time, different formats, placement arrangements, and so on. The plan is yours. We share a one-page summary of the adjustments, and only that, with the staff who need to make them.
- **Wellbeing advice.** If you are struggling, with anything, you can see an adviser. You do not need a diagnosis or a reason.
- **Referrals.** To the counselling service, to your GP, to specialist services, with your agreement.
- **Placement support.** If you are on a regulated programme and something about placement worries you, tell us before it starts.

## How to reach us

Extension 2300, or 01632 960240 from outside. wellbeing@stonegrove.ac.example. The Cloister Block, ground floor, east door. Drop-in 12:00 to 14:00 Monday to Thursday. Out of hours: the number on the back of your student card.

## What happens when you contact us

You get an appointment within ten working days, sooner if it is urgent. You meet an adviser. You talk. You decide what, if anything, you want to happen next.

## What we will not do

Tell your tutor you came, unless you ask us to. Share your diagnosis with teaching or placement staff, unless you ask us to. Keep records anywhere other than our own secure library. Judge you.

## If you are worried about someone else

Tell us. You can do that without giving your name. We will decide what to do and we will not tell them it was you.
