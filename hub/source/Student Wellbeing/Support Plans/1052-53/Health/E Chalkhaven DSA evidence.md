## Conclusion

Enia presents with a profile consistent with a specific learning difference affecting reading fluency, working memory and the speed of written output. Her verbal reasoning and comprehension are in the above-average range. The discrepancy between her underlying ability and her performance on timed literacy tasks is significant and is of long standing; she reports difficulties with reading speed throughout schooling that were not formally assessed.

## Recommendations for higher education

1. Additional time of 25% in timed written assessments.
2. Use of a computer for written examinations, with spell-check enabled.
3. Access to lecture materials in advance.
4. Permission to record teaching sessions.
5. Consideration of extensions on written coursework.
6. Marking for content rather than spelling and grammar where these are not the object of assessment.

## Note

Enia disclosed during assessment that she was anxious about placement documentation on a health programme. This is outside the scope of this assessment but the University may wish to consider placement adjustments in discussion with her.
