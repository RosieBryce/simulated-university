# INC-0031: note of the review meeting, 26 February 1053

Present: Velra Cinderwake (Head of Student Wellbeing, chair); Caelio Driftfeather (Wellbeing Adviser); Elenna Dewscroll (Information Governance Manager and DPO).

Held under the Data Breach Procedure, section 3. Not a disciplinary meeting. Nothing in this note goes to a personal file.

## What happened

On 19 February the adviser, responding to a Faculty request for all Year 2 Nursing and Care adjustments summaries within four working days, sent nine summaries in a batch to the NC-Y2-Teaching distribution list. For one student he attached the full support plan instead of the summary. The two files sit in the same folder in his working area with near-identical names. He did not check the attachment. He reported the error within the hour.

## Two separate failures

1. The wrong attachment. An error under time pressure, made possible by the way files are organised.
2. Sending to a distribution list at all. The procedure says named staff. The Faculty's request asked for the list, and the procedure gave the adviser no basis to refuse.

## Contributing condition

The adviser was carrying 41 open cases against a guidance caseload of 25, and was completing the summaries before a morning of appointments. This is recorded as a contributing condition at the DPO's request, not as a mitigation the adviser asked for. The Head of Student Wellbeing has raised caseloads with the Dean repeatedly over the past year.

## The student

Informed in person by the Head of Student Wellbeing on 20 February. Upset, then angry; principal concern that placement staff would learn of a diagnosis. Follow-up with her adviser arranged. She asked that the adviser be told she knows the error was not intended.

## Actions

| # | Action | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Self-report; containment email to recipients | Adviser; DPO | Done 19 Feb |
| 2 | Copy saved to the Year 2 teaching folder on the Hub to be removed; removal to be confirmed as done, not promised, and verified by the DPO | Programme Leader; DPO to verify | Requested |
| 3 | Procedure revised: plans and summaries in separate folders with distinct name patterns; summaries generated as separate files; no batches; no distribution lists; requests for summaries come to the Head of Student Wellbeing, not to advisers | Head of Student Wellbeing | Draft by 5 Mar |
| 4 | All advisers briefed | Head of Student Wellbeing | By 3 Mar |
| 5 | Faculty told that bulk requests will not be accepted | Head of Student Wellbeing | Done (email to Dean 20 Feb); to restate at FMG |
| 6 | Caseload recorded as contributing condition; raised again with the Dean | Head of Student Wellbeing | Ongoing |

A separate conversation about the adviser's caseload and wellbeing took place after the recording stopped and is not minuted.
