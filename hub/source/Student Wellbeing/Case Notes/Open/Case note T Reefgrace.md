# Case note

**CONFIDENTIAL. Student Wellbeing case record.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Student | Tivan Reefgrace, 1052-ED-0141, Education Studies Year 2 |
| Adviser | Caelio Driftfeather |
| Referral | Dr S. Emberthorn, Head of Department, 12 November 1052, following a focus group. Student consented to referral and to the referrer's account being passed on. |

## 14 November 1052: first meeting

Tivan attended. Quiet, embarrassed about "making a scene" in the focus group. Described falling behind since the start of semester: two late submissions, avoiding his personal tutor, poor sleep, low mood most days, no appetite in the mornings. Commutes from home on the Palm Coast, about ninety minutes each way; first in his family at university; parents proud and, he feels, watching. Said "I'm not sure I should be here" and, when asked, that he meant the University rather than anything more serious. Asked directly about risk; none disclosed, and I am satisfied on that.

Agreed: referral to the counselling service (Tivan agreed); a conversation with his tutor, which I offered to set up and attend; a short-term plan of fortnightly check-ins with me; and information about the hardship fund for travel.

Did not want a support plan at this stage. Does not have a diagnosis and did not want to pursue one now. Offered to revisit.

## 28 November 1052: check-in

Counselling first session attended. Met his tutor with me on 21 November; tutor was kind and practical, agreed a catch-up schedule for the two late pieces. Tivan slept better that week. Travel fund application submitted.

## 12 December 1052: check-in

Both late pieces submitted. Feedback workshop in the department helped ("Jaskel is a lot but he's good"). Travel fund awarded. Sleep variable. Mood better. Agreed to move to monthly.

## 20 January 1053: check-in

Semester 2 started. Anxious about placement but attended the briefing. Counselling ongoing. Asked whether the Head of Department knew what he had said to me; told him no, and that she had not asked. He was relieved and then said he did not mind her knowing he was all right.

## 10 February 1053: check-in

Doing all right. Placement started well. Asked to close the case or move to "contact if needed". Agreed to keep open until the end of semester 2 with a check-in in April, then review.
