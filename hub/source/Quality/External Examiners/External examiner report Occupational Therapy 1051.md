## 1. Standards

Appropriate and comparable. No change from last year.

## 2. Student performance

Good overall. I was pleased to see a stronger cohort of dissertations than last year. Placement portfolios remain a strength.

## 3. Assessment and marking

Accurate and well moderated. I agreed with all marks in my sample of forty-two.

## 4. Matters for the programme team's attention

I note once again that Year 2 carries twelve summative assessment points. The programme team's response to my report last year stated that the matter was "noted and under review". I would be grateful to know what the review concluded, since the assessment pattern in the Year 2 module descriptors I received this year is unchanged. The students' own comments on the matter are, if anything, more pointed than last year. I would ask the team to treat this as a recommendation rather than an observation.

I also note a small inconsistency in the marking of one Year 2 module, where one marker's marks sit consistently a few points below the moderated marks. This has been addressed in moderation and I raise it only so that the team can keep an eye on it.

## 5. Examination Board

Well conducted.

## 6. Response

I look forward to the programme team's response to section 4.

M. Boughwitness
