# External Examiner's Annual Report

| | |
|---|---|
| Institution | Stonegrove University |
| Programme | BSc (Hons) Occupational Therapy |
| External examiner | Dr Marenel Boughwitness, Elder Grove University |
| Academic year | 1051-52 |
| Year of tenure | 3 of 4 |
| Date | 30 July 1052 |

## 1. Standards

Appropriate and comparable with those at Elder Grove and elsewhere.

## 2. Student performance

Good. The dissertations were again strong. I was less sure of the Year 2 coursework this year, not because of its quality but because of its quantity: several students' work across the year read as if written in haste, and the reflective accounts say as much.

## 3. Assessment and marking

Marking is accurate at programme level. I return to the matter I raised last year concerning one marker on a Year 2 module whose marks sit below the moderated marks. The gap is wider this year than last and moderation adjusted eleven of fourteen marks. I do not doubt the marker's judgement of the work; I doubt their calibration to the programme's criteria, and I would ask that the programme team address this directly rather than through moderation, which treats the symptom. I am content for this paragraph to be treated as reserved business if the team prefers.

## 4. Matters for the programme team's attention

I note once again, for the third year, that Year 2 carries twelve summative assessment points, and that the programme's responses to my two previous reports have each stated that the matter is under review. I would ask, with respect, that the review conclude. I understand that the programme is approaching revalidation and I would hope that the revalidation panel will consider this.

## 5. Examination Board

Well conducted. I would add that the Board's papers arrived four days late this year, which I mention only because it has not happened before.

## 6. Response

I look forward to the programme team's response, and, I hope, to a different one.

M. Boughwitness
