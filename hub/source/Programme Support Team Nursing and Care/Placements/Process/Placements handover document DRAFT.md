## 3. Tabs 2 to 4: Allocation

How I actually do it:

1. Hesta exports the student list from the record system with pathway and Year 1 placement.
2. I add the home area column by hand from what students told me on the form (most didn't) or from last year.
3. I add a Wellbeing column: Y if Wellbeing have sent an adjustments summary, with a one-word note (nights, mentor, travel). Never the detail. The summary goes to the mentor through me, not through the spreadsheet.
4. I sort by pathway and go through the providers in order of how awkward they are (awkward first, because they have the most conditions) and put names against places.
5. I check nobody is going back to their Year 1 setting.
6. I check the Capacity tab by eye.
7. I send the emails (section 5).
8. Something changes and I do it again.

Rules of thumb: don't put two first-semester Year 2s together at a small provider; don't send anyone who can't drive to Brinestep or Cragburrow without a travel bursary; the mental health pathway students go to Millbrook or Riverside first if possible; Stonebridge only takes two on a shift so check the shift pattern.
