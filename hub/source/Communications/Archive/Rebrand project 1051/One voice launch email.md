Subject: One voice: we are Stonegrove University

Dear colleagues

From today, we are Stonegrove University.

The name on the gate has changed, and so has everything that carries it: the website, the prospectus, the signage, the templates you will find in Communications / Brand on the Hub. One logo, one palette, two typefaces, one way of writing. A short guide explains it all and I would love it if you read it; it takes ten minutes.

Why. Because we are one institution. Elves and Dwarves, five faculties, fifty-five programmes, and until today, five letterheads. The College of the Vale was a fine name for what we were. Stonegrove University is the name for what we are.

What this means for you. Use the new templates from today. Retire the old ones, wherever you have them. Use the new name, and the new names for faculties and departments, which are in the guide. If you need anything made, ask Communications; please do not make it yourself. If you are not sure, ask.

Gentle reminder: the old crest, the old wordmark and the old names belong to our history now. We will treat them with respect and we will not use them.

Thank you to everyone on the project board and to Sorell Inkthread, who wrote the guide and designed the logo and will, I suspect, be keeping a close eye on all of us.

One voice.

Rhialla
