# Stonegrove University: brand on one page

**Name.** Stonegrove University. Then "the University". Never "College of the Vale", never "Stonegrove Uni". Departments by their official names; "Healing Arts" and "Pedagogy" were retired in 1051.

**Logo.** Coat of arms plus wordmark, supplied files only. Clear space: the height of the shield, all sides. Minimum 30 mm or 120 px. No sub-logos, ever: ask Communications for a lock-up.

**Colour.** Parchment #f5f0e0, Deep Parchment #ede8dc, Navy #1a1a2e, Gold #c9a84c, Grove Green #7a9e82. Ember Coral #d98878 for small accents only. No gradients, no shadows.

**Type.** Playfair Display for headings, Source Sans 3 for everything else. Two faces on a page, never more. Sentence case.

**Voice.** Warm, plain, confident. Short sentences. British spelling. No exclamation marks in official communications; one in a social post at most. Students are students.

**Hashtag.** #Stonegrove.

**Templates.** Brand / Templates. Anything older than September 1051 is withdrawn.

**The five never-dos.** Your own logo. The old name. The logo on a gradient. A novelty typeface. Two exclamation marks.

Communications, extension 2400. Ask before you make.
