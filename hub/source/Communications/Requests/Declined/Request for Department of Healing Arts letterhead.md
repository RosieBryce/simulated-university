# MEMORANDUM

**From:** Dr Hrogar Dourstone, Head of the Department of Healing Arts
**To:** The Head of Communications
**Copy:** The Dean
**Date:** 14.03.53
**Subject:** Departmental letterhead (second request)

1. I wrote on 3 October 1051 requesting that Communications supply the Department with letterhead bearing its name and the University crest, in the manner of the letterhead the Department has used since 1012. I received a reply declining the request on the ground that the Department's name had been changed.

2. I am minded to renew the request. The Department of Healing Arts has borne that name for forty-one years. Its graduates know it by that name. Its placement providers address correspondence to it by that name. The decision to rename it was taken by a project board on which the Department was not represented, and was communicated to the Department after the event.

3. I do not ask Communications to reverse the decision. I ask that the Department be permitted to use its name on its own correspondence, as a matter of courtesy to its history, alongside the University's.

4. I should be grateful for a reply before the Faculty Management Group next meets.

H. Dourstone

---

*Communications response, 17 March 1053 (S. Inkthread, cc R. Moondrift):*

*Dear Dr Dourstone. Thank you for your memorandum. I am sorry to decline a second time, and I want to explain rather than simply refuse. The brand guide (section 2.3) retires "Department of Healing Arts" and names "Department of Health" as the official name; the guide was approved by Senate in July 1051 and Communications does not have discretion to vary it for one department. I recognise that the renaming was poorly consulted and I have said so to the Head of Communications. What I can offer, and would like to: a lock-up carrying "Department of Health" in the brand typeface with the University logo, which is what every other department uses; and, if the Department wishes to mark its history, a line in the footer of departmental documents reading "Founded as the Department of Healing Arts, 1012". That is permitted under section 2.2 as historical use with a date. I would be glad to produce both this week. With respect, S. Inkthread, Brand and Design Officer.*

*Logged BCL-020. Declined. No reply received.*
