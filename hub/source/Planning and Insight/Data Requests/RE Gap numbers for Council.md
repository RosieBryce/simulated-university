Dear Cyranor

Happy to. Before Erra cuts anything I would be cautious about four things, and I would rather raise them now than in the Council Chamber.

1. Which gap. I assume the Elf to Dwarf good honours gap, as in the annual report. If Council means something else (progression, continuation, grades), say so, because the answers differ.

2. Which year. The most recent graduates we can report on are 1052 (completed 1051-52). The 1053 graduates will not exist until the summer boards. So "the latest figure" for a May paper is the one Council already saw in November: {{ he_good_degree_gap_1052 }} percentage points. If the paper quotes anything else as current, it is wrong.

3. By programme. The denominator here is the problem. Each programme graduates somewhere between fifty and a hundred and thirty students a year; split by species, a few cells fall below twenty and we suppress them. A "by programme" table will have gaps in it, and the programmes with the smallest cohorts will swing by ten points from one year to the next for no reason at all. I would rather Council understood why than wondered what we were hiding, and I would rather the Faculty did not set programme targets on single-year figures.

4. What Council actually asked for. Aerion's note, as I understand it, was for two pages: figures, what the Faculty will do, how you will know. The figures part is a paragraph and a five-year table. We can supply that in a day. The other two parts are yours, and I would gently suggest they are the harder bit.

Erra's extract from October is half done; she was pulled onto the NSS work. She can finish it by the 20th if that suits.

Subject to the caveats above, of course.
