# Admissions tone of voice: how we write to applicants

Mira. Updated August 1052.

Applicants are nervous, hopeful, and comparing us with somewhere else. Everything we send should make them feel wanted. Here's how.

1. **Be upbeat!!** Exclamation marks are friendly. One is good, two is better. We are delighted, thrilled, absolutely delighted. Never "pleased".
2. **Use their first name.** Every time.
3. **Say "we can't wait".** Because we can't!!
4. **Describe the place.** Forest meets mountain. Nowhere like it. Paint the picture.
5. **Always a next step.** Offer holder day, campus visit, a call. Never end a letter without one.
6. **Sign off warmly.** "With very best wishes" not "Regards". (Bruk, this means you.)
7. **Keep the College name.** "Stonegrove College of the Vale" is what applicants' parents remember. The University thing is fine for the website but applicants respond to the old name.

What Comms say: sentence case, no exclamation marks, "Stonegrove University", plain and confident. I've read the brand guide. I think it's a bit cold for admissions. We're the warm bit!!

M.
