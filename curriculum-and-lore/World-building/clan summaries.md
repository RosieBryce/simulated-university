## 🛠️ **Dwarf Clans – Key Cultural Traits**

| Clan          | Key Cultural Points                                                                    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Malachite** | Industrious smiths and artisans; value resilience, craftsmanship, and kinship.         |
| **Granite**   | Stoic and hierarchical; tradition-bound warriors and civic leaders.                    |
| **Flint**     | Gritty, tough, and blunt; miners, frontline workers, and pragmatists.                  |
| **Alabaster** | Marginalised, dignified, and enduring; often in lower-status roles, value community.   |
| **Sandstone** | Adaptable and upbeat; traders, messengers, and builders of bridges (literal & social). |
| **Obsidian**  | Mysterious, elegant, and philosophical; spiritual leaders and lorekeepers.             |
| **Bismuth**   | Iridescent and enigmatic; creatives, polymaths, and eccentric thinkers.                |
| **Slate**     | Quiet, brittle, and focused; planners, engineers, and those who prefer precision.      |

## 🍃 **Elf Clans – Key Cultural Traits**

| Clan       | Key Cultural Points                                                                   |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Holly**  | Scholarly and devout; caretakers of knowledge and ritual, balanced by warmth.         |
| **Yew**    | Stern, ancient, and politically savvy; guardians of long memory and cultural power.   |
| **Rowan**  | Expressive, curious, and poetic; nature-linked seers and storytellers.                |
| **Ash**    | Resilient and passionate; storm-walkers who see rebirth through fire and adversity.   |
| **Baobab** | Majestic and powerful; deeply rooted elites with ceremonial grace and social control. |
| **Palm**   | Joyful yet tragic; seafaring survivors who turn hardship into celebration and beauty. |
