### Cultural Shift: The Role of Dwarven Women in Higher Education
Dwarven women, far from being passive, are **matrons, craftswomen, lawkeepers, and strategists**. The **Stoneborn Institute** ensures that knowledge **isn’t just about war and metal**, but about **heritage, home, and leadership**.

- **Mothers of the Hall** – Senior scholars who **oversee student disputes, organise traditions, and ensure the wisdom of the past is not forgotten**.
- **Hearthwardens** – Protectors of **dwarven hearths and halls**, blending magic, defensive tactics, and diplomacy.
- **Runekeepers & Loremasters** – Those who **preserve dwarven history through music, sculpture, and archives**.
