At a Glance

Stonegrove University is a fictional institution set in a Middle-Earth-inspired world. The dataset covers seven academic years of simulated student lifecycle data — from enrolment to graduate outcomes.

Enrolment over time

Active enrolments per academic year. Numbers grow as each new cohort enters while continuing students remain.

Student Population

35,000 unique students across seven cohorts. Two species — Elf and Dwarf — drawn from 14 clans, with a range of socioeconomic backgrounds, disabilities, and first-generation statuses.

Species

Note: the Dwarf–Elf split is approximately 60/40, not 50/50. This reflects intentional variation in clan sizes.

Gender

"Neuter" appears for a subset of both Elf and Dwarf students, in keeping with the Middle-Earth lore setting.

Clans

14 clans in total — 8 Dwarf, 6 Elf. Clan membership shapes personality profiles and, indirectly, attainment outcomes through socioeconomic and motivational pathways.

ClanSpeciesStudents% of species

Socioeconomic rank

Ranked 1 (lowest) to 8 (highest). SES is a key driver of the attainment gap — it influences prior educational experience, disability likelihood, and first-generation status.

Disabilities

Students may have multiple recorded disabilities. Percentages show the share of students with any mention of each category.

First-generation students

First-generation status is SES-weighted: students from lower SES backgrounds are more likely to be the first in their family to attend university. This variable is available in dim_students as a boolean.

Programmes & Faculties

55 programmes across 5 faculties. Each programme runs across Years 1, 2, and 3, with different module sets per year. Programme codes use the format faculty_level_programme (e.g. 1_2_1).

Stonegrove University is a synthetic dataset. All data is fictional.  |  stonegrove.ac
