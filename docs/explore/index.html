Overview

Who are Stonegrove's students? Seven cohorts across 7 academic years, two species, 14 clans, 55 programmes. This tab summarises the student population and university structure. Use it to orient yourself before exploring the data.

Species

The Dwarf–Elf split is approximately 60/40, not 50/50 — this reflects variation in clan sizes.

Gender

"Neuter" appears for a subset of both Elf and Dwarf students, in keeping with the Middle-Earth lore setting.

Enrolment by academic year

Active enrolments grow each year as new cohorts enter while continuing students remain.

Clans

14 clans total — 8 Dwarf, 6 Elf. Clan membership shapes personality profiles and, indirectly, attainment outcomes through socioeconomic and motivational pathways.

Socioeconomic rank

Ranked 1 (lowest) to 8 (highest). SES drives prior educational experience and is a key mediator of the attainment gap.

Disabilities

Students may have multiple recorded disabilities. Figures show % of students with any mention of each category.

Programmes by faculty

55 programmes across 5 faculties, each running Years 1–3.

Engagement

Weekly attendance across the 12-week semester, averaged across all modules a student takes. Academic year filter applies. Attendance does not vary meaningfully by faculty — the pattern is driven by species and individual factors.

Attendance Through the Year

Mean attendance rate (proportion of timetabled sessions attended) by week and species. Attendance is highest in the first two weeks and dips slightly around mid-semester, before stabilising through to the end of term.

Wellbeing

Data from two surveys: the annual enrolment survey (all students, ~82% response rate) and the NSS satisfaction survey (Year 3 only, ~68% response rate). All filters apply.

Sense of Belonging by Year of Study

Mean belonging scores (1–5 scale) from the enrolment survey, broken down by year of study. Shows how students' connection to their programme changes as they progress. Faculty and academic year filters apply.
