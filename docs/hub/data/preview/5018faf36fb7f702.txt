# NSS 1052: Faculty of Health and Education deep dive

Planning and Insight. Version 01, 17 October 1052. Requested by the Dean following publication of NSS 1052 results. Internal.

## How to read this

- NSS 1052 surveyed final-year students in academic year 1051-52; results were published in August 1052.
- Scores are mean responses on a 1 to 5 scale, respondents only. Response counts are small at programme level and a movement of 0.1 is well within what the numbers produce by chance.
- **A Faculty figure is an average of two departments.** The two moved in different directions this year. Read the department lines before acting on the Faculty line.

## Headline

Faculty overall satisfaction: 3.79 (NSS 1051) to 3.82 (NSS 1052). Flat.

Faculty Assessment and Feedback: 3.65 to 3.62. The theme that moved, and the one the Dean's note was about.

Respondents: 815 across the twelve programmes.

## What the free text says

Free text comments on Assessment and Feedback in the Health programmes that fell (Occupational Therapy, Somatic Wellbeing Enquiry and Pharmacy most) cluster on three things: the volume of assessment in Year 2; the time taken to return feedback; and feedback that "tells me the mark but not what to do". The Occupational Therapy external examiner has recommended a reduction in Year 2 assessment load in each of the last three reports.

## What this does not say

- It does not say the Department of Education has a problem with assessment and feedback. Education's line went up. If Education wants evidence about its students below Year 3, the annual enrolment survey (all years) is the place to look, and it is flat.
- It does not say the Faculty has fallen. Overall satisfaction is unchanged.
- It does not support an action plan written at Faculty level and applied to every programme. The movement is in one department and one theme.

## Recommendation

Department of Health, and Occupational Therapy, Somatic Wellbeing Enquiry and Pharmacy in particular: review the Year 2 assessment load and feedback turnaround, which for OT is also what the external examiner has asked for. Department of Education: no action on this evidence; keep doing what you are doing.

E. Measurine
