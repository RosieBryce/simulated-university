Dear Halden

Thank you for your project request of 14 June, reference 1051-022.

Decision: not approved.

Reasons:

1. Insufficient business case. Section 1 does not describe the problem in terms Digital Services can assess (who is affected, how often, with what consequence). Section 8 does not quantify the benefit.
2. No process documentation. Section 7 is blank. Digital Services cannot specify or procure a system for a process that has not been described.
3. Requirements are not testable. "A database for placements" and "reports" cannot be built to or checked against.
4. Section 6 indicates that health information may be held. Any system holding Confidential data requires a Data Protection Impact Assessment before Digital Services can consider it, and the request should say what data is needed, not what is currently held.

Digital Services recognises that placements administration is a significant workload for the Faculty. If the team is able to document the current process, state the requirements in testable form, and quantify the problem, we would welcome a resubmission via the intake process. The Governance business case template may help.

Please resubmit via the Digital Services intake process on the Hub rather than by email.
