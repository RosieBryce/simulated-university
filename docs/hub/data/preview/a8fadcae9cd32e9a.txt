# If something goes wrong on placement

Keep this card in the back of your Practice Assessment Record.

## You are ill

1. Ring the placement area before your shift starts.
2. Email hesta.gracestone@stonegrove.ac.example the same day.
3. Make up the hours before the end of the module.

## You have made a mistake

1. Tell your mentor or the nurse in charge straight away. Patient safety comes first.
2. Write it down: what happened, when, who you told.
3. Tell Halden or Dagna that day.
4. You will not be referred for fitness to practise for making an honest mistake and reporting it. You may be if you do not report it.

## You see something that worries you

1. Tell your mentor.
2. If it is about your mentor, ring Halden: 07700 900415, or Dagna: extension 4120.
3. If nobody answers, the Programme Support Team office: 01632 960415, extension 4151.
4. You are protected when you raise a concern in good faith.

## Your adjustments are not being applied

1. Tell Halden that week.
2. Or your adviser in Student Wellbeing: extension 2300.

## You cannot cope

1. Student Wellbeing: 01632 960240, extension 2300. They do not tell us what you say.
2. Your personal tutor.
3. Out of hours: the number on the back of your student card.
