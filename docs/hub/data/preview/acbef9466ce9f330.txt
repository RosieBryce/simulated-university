## Aims

To prepare students to practise safely, competently and compassionately as registered nurses and carers; to develop the judgement to recognise the limits of their own competence; to ground practice in evidence; and to produce practitioners who write things down and tell someone when something has gone wrong.

## Learning outcomes

On completion, students will be able to:

1. Deliver fundamental care safely and with respect for the dignity of the person.
2. Assess, plan, implement and evaluate care under supervision and, by the end of Year 3, with the independence expected at the point of registration.
3. Administer medicines safely and accurately, and document administration correctly.
4. Communicate effectively with patients, families and the multidisciplinary team.
5. Recognise and escalate deterioration.
6. Practise within the professional code and the law.
7. Reflect on their own practice and act on feedback.

## Assessment

A mix of written examinations, coursework, practical skills assessments (OSCEs), placement records and reflective portfolios. Placement components are pass or fail and must be passed. The medicines calculation component of NC202 has a pass mark of 100%.

## Placements

Placements are allocated by the Programme Support Team across approximately thirty providers. Students may not arrange or swap placements. Occupational health clearance and mandatory training are prerequisites.

## Admission

Standard University entry requirements plus a satisfactory health declaration and enhanced vetting. Applicants are interviewed. Contextual offers are made in line with University policy.

## Progression and award

Students must pass all modules to progress and to be awarded. The honours classification is determined under Regulation D3 (Year 2 one third, Year 3 two thirds). A student who leaves after Year 2 having passed 240 credits is eligible for the Diploma of Higher Education as an exit award, which does not lead to registration.

## Fitness to practise

Students are subject to the University's Fitness to Practise Procedure throughout.
