# Placements: the process

One slide per step. Attempt two. Sept 1052.

---

# Step 1: Ask providers

- June: letter to all providers
- Ask: how many, what pathway, what conditions, anything changed
- Record replies in Providers tab (col E and F)
- Chase non-repliers in September
- Arrow to step 2

---

# Step 2: Build the list

- Student list from the record system (Hesta exports it)
- Add pathway, Year 1 placement, home area
- Add Wellbeing flags (adjustments Y/N, nothing else)
- Add student constraints from the form they fill in (hardly anyone fills it in)
- Arrow to step 3

---

# Step 3: Allocate

- Match students to places (the jigsaw)
- Check capacity tab (formulas broken, check by eye)
- Check nobody's gone back to a Year 1 placement
- Check adjustments against provider conditions (no nights etc)
- Arrow to step 4

---

# Step 4: Confirm

- Email each provider: names and start dates only
- Email each student: provider, mentor if known, start date, link to profile
- Two weeks before. Aim.
- Arrow to step 5

---

# Step 5: Pre-start checks

- OH cleared? (portal, or letter for Hollowpick)
- Mandatory training done?
- Provider extras done? (interview, briefing, boots, form, induction)
- If no: chase student, delay start, tell provider
- Arrow to step 6

---

# Step 6: During

- Weekly: any issues? (mentor, hours, conduct, wellbeing)
- Log in Issues tab
- Escalate: Dagna (conduct), Wellbeing (student), Nyssa (FtP)
- Arrow to step 7

---

# Step 7: After

- Hours confirmed by provider
- Practice Assessment Record signed
- Make-up hours arranged if needed
- Thank-you email to provider (I always do this and it matters more than people think)
- Back to step 1

---

# Step 0: When it all goes wrong

- Provider pulls out: ring Agda, then the list of providers with spare places (Capacity tab), then students in order of travel distance
- Student without clearance: send home, chase OH, restart next week, tell provider
- Swap: pull both, tell Dagna
- This slide is the one that should be a whole document
