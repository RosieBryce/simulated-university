### Summary

The Faculty of Health and Education takes the awarding gap between its Elf and Dwarf graduates with the utmost seriousness. This paper sets out the figures, the Faculty's bold and ambitious response, and how we will measure success. The Faculty is determined to be a leader in this area.

### Recommendation

Council is asked to NOTE the Faculty's commitment and to ENDORSE the actions set out below.

### 1. The figures

The gap in good honours between Elf and Dwarf graduates in the Faculty stands at 27.9 percentage points. This is a matter of real concern to me and to my colleagues.

Looking across the Faculty, 63.6% of Dwarf graduates in the Faculty achieved a good degree. We believe this is a figure the Faculty can and must improve.

The gap has narrowed over the past three years, which demonstrates that the Faculty's existing efforts are bearing fruit, but there is much more to do.

### 2. What the Faculty will do

The Faculty will be bold. Specifically:

- A Faculty Awarding Gap Taskforce, chaired by the Dean, meeting termly.
- Every programme to produce an awarding gap action plan by September 1053.
- Enhanced personal tutoring for all first-year students.
- A review of assessment practice across the Faculty, building on the work already under way in Occupational Therapy and the Department of Education.
- Placement allocation to take account of students' circumstances [Dagna: is this right?]
- Staff development on inclusive teaching.

### 3. How we will know

The Faculty will set a target of halving the gap within three years and will report progress to Council annually through the Planning and Insight annual report.
